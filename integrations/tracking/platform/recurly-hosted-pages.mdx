`Difficulty: Easy` <br/>
`Developer Required: No`

With FirstPromoter, you can setup referral tracking using our `Recurly Hosted Pages` integration option. This is only needed if you use Recurly's hosted checkout pages — this setup mainly requires you to add/insert our ready made script into your thank-you page.

Click [here](https://login.firstpromoter.com?redirect=%2Finstructions%2Frecurly-hosted-pages) to view the guide.
