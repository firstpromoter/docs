`Difficulty: Easy` <br/>
`Developer Required: No`

With FirstPromoter, you can setup click and referral tracking using our WebinarJam integration option.
This setup requires adding our ready made scripts to your landing and thank-you/confirmation pages, along with a custom confirmation page redirect in WebinarJam to pass the registrant's email to your thank-you page.

Click [here](https://login.firstpromoter.com?redirect=%2Finstructions%2Fwebinar-jam) to view the guide.
