With FirstPromoter, you can setup click and referral tracking using our Jotform integration option.
This setup requires adding our ready made scripts to your landing and thank-you pages, along with a redirect in Jotform to pass the submitted email to your thank-you page.

Click [here](https://login.firstpromoter.com?redirect=%2Finstructions%2Fjotform) to view the guide.
