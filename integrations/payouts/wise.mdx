<Note>
  **Beta.** The Wise payout integration is available as a beta feature and appears with a **Beta** label in
  **Settings → Integrations**. The setup flow and field names described here may still change.
</Note>

# What it does

Wise lets you send money to people in most countries, in their own currency. If you already pay your affiliates from a
Wise Business account, this integration removes the manual round trip: instead of exporting payouts from FirstPromoter,
building a batch payment in Wise and marking everything as paid by hand, FirstPromoter creates the transfers in your
Wise account for you and keeps each payout's status in sync.

Once connected:

- Promoters **connect their own Wise account** from the affiliate portal, the same way they would connect Stripe.
  FirstPromoter reads the bank account details Wise issued them and pays those — no bank numbers are typed anywhere.
- Paying a batch of payouts becomes a **one-click** action in FirstPromoter. Every payout run creates one batch of
  transfers in your Wise account, which you pay from your Wise balance with a single approval in Wise.
- Payout statuses (sent, cancelled, refunded) flow back into FirstPromoter automatically.

<Warning>
  FirstPromoter never holds or moves your money. Payments are made **by your own Wise Business account, from your own
  balance** — FirstPromoter only creates the transfers and tracks what Wise reports back. Funding, currency conversion,
  compliance and Wise's fees stay between you and Wise.
</Warning>

# Requirements

| What you need | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| A **Wise Business account** | [wise.com/business](https://wise.com/business). A personal Wise account cannot be used to pay promoters through the integration |
| **Authorisation for FirstPromoter** | FirstPromoter is a Wise partner application. You grant access by clicking **Connect Wise** in FirstPromoter and approving the request in Wise — no API keys or personal tokens are copied by hand |
| A **business profile** | After connecting, you pick which of your Wise profiles the payouts are sent from. Only business profiles are offered. If your Wise login has exactly one business profile, FirstPromoter selects it for you |
| A funded **balance** in your payout currency | Batches are funded from your Wise balance in your FirstPromoter account currency. Each promoter receives exactly the payout amount; Wise's fee is added on top and taken from the same balance, so keep a little headroom. Top it up in Wise before a payout run |
| The right **environment** | **Sandbox** connects to Wise's test environment (no real money moves), **Production** to your live Wise account. The two are completely separate accounts |

<Tip>
  Sandbox is there to try the flow end to end — connect, add a promoter, run a payout — without moving real money.
  Nothing carries over when you switch to production: profiles, recipients and transfers all live in one environment
  only.
</Tip>

# Connecting Wise in FirstPromoter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the integration">
    In FirstPromoter, go to **Settings → Integrations** and open the **Wise** card (labelled **Beta**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the environment">
    Select **Sandbox** or **Production**. You can switch later, but each environment needs its own authorisation and
    its own profile selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Wise">
    Click **Connect Wise**. You are taken to Wise, where you sign in and approve FirstPromoter's access to your
    account. Wise then sends you back to FirstPromoter and the card shows **Connected**.

    FirstPromoter stores only the access Wise grants it — your Wise password and two-factor codes are entered on
    Wise's own pages and are never seen by FirstPromoter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the business profile">
    Choose the Wise **profile** that payouts should be sent from. The list is read from your Wise account; use
    **Refresh** if you have just created a profile in Wise. A single business profile is selected automatically.

    <Warning>
      Pick the profile whose balance you intend to pay from. Changing the connected profile after promoters have been
      set up is not supported without re-creating their recipients — contact support if you need to move to a
      different profile.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test the connection">
    Click **Test connection**. FirstPromoter calls Wise to confirm the authorisation is still valid, that the selected
    profile is still visible and that it can read your balances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Click **Enable**. For your security, enabling a payout integration requires a two-factor verification step.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Offer it to promoters">
    Wise now appears in **Payouts → Payout terms** as an available payout method. Enable it there for it to show up in
    your promoters' affiliate portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# Keeping statuses up to date

FirstPromoter keeps payouts in sync with Wise in two ways, and you do not have to configure either of them.

- **Polling.** After a batch is sent, FirstPromoter asks Wise for the state of every transfer that is still in flight —
  first after a couple of minutes, then with a growing gap (minutes, then hours) while nothing changes. Polling
  continues for up to **30 days**; a transfer that has still not reached a final state by then is marked as failed so
  it stops blocking the payout.
- **Webhooks.** When you enable the integration, FirstPromoter subscribes to Wise's transfer state-change
  notifications for the selected profile automatically. The **webhook URL** is shown read-only on the Wise card in
  FirstPromoter, so you can see where the events go. There is nothing to register in Wise by hand, and the
  subscription is removed again when you disconnect.

Every incoming event is signature-verified against Wise's public key, and repeated deliveries of the same event are
ignored, so Wise's automatic retries are safe.

<Note>
  Wise does not emit a state-change notification for every kind of transfer, which is why polling — not the webhook —
  is what FirstPromoter relies on. A missing webhook never leaves a payout stuck; it only makes the update arrive a
  little later.
</Note>

# How promoters connect

Promoters connect their own Wise account themselves, from the affiliate portal. Any Wise account works — **personal or
business** — and it does not have to be in the same country or currency as yours.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open payout methods">
    The promoter goes to **My payouts → Payout methods** and adds **Wise**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect Wise">
    They click **Connect Wise** and are taken to Wise, where they log in and approve FirstPromoter's access. If their
    Wise login has more than one profile, they choose the one they want to be paid into.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Back to the portal">
    Wise sends them back to the affiliate portal. FirstPromoter reads the **account details** Wise issued for that
    profile and turns them into a recipient in your Wise account, so payouts reach them as an ordinary bank transfer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **What FirstPromoter stores.** Only what Wise hands over: which Wise profile was connected, the account details Wise
  issued for it (shown back to the promoter masked, for example *USD · Checking ····1234*) and the recipient created
  from them in your Wise account. The promoter never types an IBAN, routing number or account number into
  FirstPromoter, and neither you nor FirstPromoter can see or act on their Wise account beyond reading those details.
</Note>

## The promoter needs active account details

Wise issues **account details** — the bank details other people can send money to — per currency, and only once the
account is verified. A promoter can only be paid when their connected profile has **active account details in a
currency you can pay**: ideally your account currency, otherwise another active currency, which Wise converts into on
the way.

If Wise has not issued any yet, the payout method stays **Pending** with a *details not issued* note. The promoter
opens Wise → **Account details**, gets the details issued for the currency they want (Wise may ask them to verify their
identity or make a first top-up), and then presses **Refresh** on the payout method in the portal. FirstPromoter reads
the details again and the card flips to **Ready**.

Statuses shown on the payout method:

| Badge | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | The Wise account is linked, FirstPromoter is reading the account details |
| **Pending** | Wise has not issued usable account details for this profile yet — the reason is shown on the card, with a **Refresh** button to check again |
| **Ready** | A recipient was created from the promoter's account details. They can be included in payouts |

As with any payout method, one that you disable in FirstPromoter is excluded from payouts — that is a FirstPromoter
state and nothing is changed in the promoter's Wise account.

## How a promoter disconnects

The promoter removes the **Wise** payout method in **My payouts → Payout methods**. That deletes the connection and the
stored details from FirstPromoter; nothing in their Wise account changes and payouts already on their way are still
delivered by Wise. They can also revoke FirstPromoter's access at any time from their Wise account settings — the
payout method then stops working and shows as needing to be connected again.

# Paying promoters

Paying works like any other one-click payout method:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Generate the payouts">
    Approve commissions and generate payouts as usual.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select and pay">
    In **Payouts**, select the Wise payouts and start the payment. FirstPromoter creates **one batch of transfers in
    your Wise account per FirstPromoter batch**, one transfer per promoter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the batch in Wise">
    The batch waits in FirstPromoter with a **Pay in Wise** button. Open it, pay the batch in your Wise account, and
    FirstPromoter takes over again from there — see below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch the statuses">
    Once the batch is paid, it goes to **processing** while Wise works through it, and each payout settles on its own
    as Wise reports back.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **You pay the batch in Wise — this is the normal step, not an error.** Wise requires the business itself to approve
  the payment with **strong customer authentication**, which is the extra confirmation EU and UK account holders give
  in their own Wise account or app. A server cannot do that on your behalf, so FirstPromoter prepares the whole batch
  and then hands it to you.

  The batch sits as **waiting for funds** with a **Pay in Wise** button that opens it in your Wise account, under
  **Payments → Batch payments**. Pay it there — topping up your balance first if it does not cover the batch — and
  FirstPromoter picks the change up on its next check and moves the batch to **processing** by itself. Nothing has to
  be re-sent from FirstPromoter, and the transfers are never created twice.
</Note>

What the statuses mean:

| Wise transfer status | Status in FirstPromoter |
| --- | --- |
| `outgoing_payment_sent` | **Completed** — Wise has sent the money to the promoter |
| `cancelled` | **Failed** — the transfer was cancelled |
| `funds_refunded` | **Failed** — Wise returned the money to your balance |
| `charged_back` | **Failed** — the payment was charged back |
| `incoming_payment_waiting`, `incoming_payment_initiated` | **Processing** — Wise is waiting for the transfer to be funded |
| `processing` | **Processing** — Wise is working on the transfer |
| `funds_converted` | **Processing** — the currency conversion is done, the payout is on its way |
| `bounced_back` | **Processing**, with an alert on the payout — Wise could not deliver and is retrying or returning the money |
| `waiting_recipient_input_to_proceed` | **Processing**, with an alert on the payout — Wise is waiting on something from the recipient before it can continue |

<Tip>
  FirstPromoter only marks a payout as **failed** for the three statuses above, because those are the only ones where
  Wise has definitively stopped. Anything else stays **processing** with the Wise status shown on the payout, so a
  payout Wise may still deliver is never double-paid by mistake.
</Tip>

Failed payouts stay selectable — fix the cause in Wise (or with the promoter) and include them in a new payout run.

Promoters who have not connected their Wise account, or whose account details are not usable yet, are not sent to Wise
at all. Their payouts are marked as failed with the reason, so you can see exactly who still has to finish connecting.

# Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Test connection fails">
    - **Not connected** — the authorisation is missing or was revoked in Wise. Click **Connect Wise** again.
    - **Invalid credentials** — Wise rejected the stored authorisation. This normally means it was revoked from your
      Wise account's app permissions; reconnect to fix it.
    - **Profile not found** — the selected profile is no longer visible to the connected Wise login (it was removed, or
      you reconnected with a different account). Refresh the profile list and pick one again.
    - **Wise error** — Wise rejected the request for another reason; the message shown comes straight from Wise.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A batch is waiting on “Pay in Wise”">
    That is the expected state for a new batch — Wise asks the business itself for something a server cannot provide:

    - **Strong customer authentication (SCA).** Accounts in the EU and the UK confirm payments with their own second
      factor. Open the batch with **Pay in Wise** and pay it in your Wise account or app, under **Payments → Batch
      payments**.
    - **Insufficient balance.** If the batch is larger than the balance in your payout currency, top up in Wise first,
      then pay the batch.

    Once it is paid, FirstPromoter detects it automatically — no re-run is needed. Cancelling the batch in Wise instead
    will mark the payouts as failed in FirstPromoter on the next check, and they can be paid again in a new run.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A promoter's payout method stays Pending">
    **Pending** means Wise has not given FirstPromoter usable account details for the connected profile yet. The card
    shows the reason; the two common ones are:

    - **Details not issued.** Wise has not issued account details for that profile in any currency you can pay. The
      promoter opens Wise → **Account details**, gets them issued for the currency they want — Wise may first ask them
      to verify their identity or top the account up — and then presses **Refresh** on the payout method.
    - **Details not usable yet.** Wise returned details it will not yet accept as a recipient, which normally means the
      account is still being verified. This is not the promoter's mistake and there is nothing to correct; **Refresh**
      once Wise finishes.

    A promoter whose payout method is not **Ready** at the moment you pay is not sent to Wise at all — their payout is
    marked as failed with the reason, so you can see exactly who still has to finish.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Unsupported currency or destination">
    Promoters are paid into the account details Wise issued them, in their currency — FirstPromoter prefers details in
    your account currency and otherwise lets Wise convert. If a promoter's only account details are in a currency Wise
    cannot pay from your balance, or Wise rejects the route, the payout fails with Wise's message and they should be
    paid with a different payout method. Your FirstPromoter account currency also has to be one Wise can hold as a
    balance, because batches are funded from that balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A payout stays processing">
    First check what Wise itself says about the transfer — the Wise status is shown on the payout. `bounced_back` and
    `waiting_recipient_input_to_proceed` (Wise is waiting on the recipient for something) are both normal "not finished
    yet" states and usually resolve on their own.

    FirstPromoter re-checks in-flight transfers on a widening schedule for up to 30 days, so a missed webhook only
    delays an update, it never loses it. A transfer that is still not final after 30 days is marked as failed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Disconnecting Wise">
    **Disconnect** in the Wise card revokes FirstPromoter's access, removes the webhook subscription, clears the stored
    authorisation and profile selection, and turns the payout method off. For your security, disconnecting requires a
    two-factor verification step.

    Disconnecting does not touch anything in your Wise account: recipients, transfers already created and your balance
    all stay exactly as they are, and transfers in flight are still paid by Wise. Promoters keep their own Wise
    connection, so re-enabling the integration puts them back in the payout runs — a promoter only has to act if their
    payout method shows **Pending** afterwards, where **Refresh** is enough.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Moving from sandbox to production">
    Switching the environment does **not** carry anything over — Wise's sandbox and live environments are separate
    accounts with separate profiles, recipients and transfers. To go live:

    1. Switch the environment to **Production** on the Wise card.
    2. Click **Connect Wise** and authorise with your live Wise Business account.
    3. Pick the business profile you want to pay from and run **Test connection**.
    4. Click **Enable**. Promoters then connect their Wise account again from the portal, because a connection made in
       sandbox exists only there.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I keep using Wise manually as well?">
    Yes. Transfers and batches you create yourself in Wise are unaffected — FirstPromoter only tracks the transfers it
    created itself. Batches sent from FirstPromoter are named after the FirstPromoter payout batch, so they are easy to
    recognise in your Wise activity.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
