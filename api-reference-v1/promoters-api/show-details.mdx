---
title: 'Show promoter details and balance'
description: "Get a promoter's details, identified by id, cust_id, auth_token, email or ref_id (legacy v1 API)."
openapi: 'openapi-promoters GET /show'
---

You can identify promoters by: `id` , `cust_id` , `auth_token` , `promoter_email` or `ref_id`(referral id). You need to pass at least one of these parameters.
